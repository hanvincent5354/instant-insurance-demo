# Etiqa Instant Travel Insurance (Instant Insurance Toggle) — Brag Launch Showcase

An AI-driven insurtech solution created with Maybank and Etiqa.

When customers purchase their flight ticket using Maybank, the bank detects the flight event and prompts them to forward their booking itinerary email to `travel@etiqa.com.my`. The Etiqa AI engine parses the itinerary in 1.2 seconds, automatically configures the best travel protection policy, and delivers a 1-tap pre-filled checkout link.

---

## What's in this Project

1. **Launch Video Reel & Storyboard** (`brag-output/brag-plan.md` and `index.html` player)
   - 20-second animated video reel showcasing the 5 core customer journey beats.
   - Built-in Web Audio API sound effects (Maybank push notification ping, AI scan laser, payment chime, and policy celebration).

2. **Interactive Customer Journey Sandbox**
   - Configure custom airline flights (AirAsia, Malaysia Airlines, Singapore Airlines), destinations (Tokyo, London, Bangkok, etc.), trip durations, and traveler counts.
   - Experience live calculation across Silver, Gold, and Platinum coverage tiers.
   - Test the complete flow: *Flight Transaction Detected ➔ Forward Email ➔ AI Parsing ➔ 1-Tap Policy Active*.

3. **Customer Validation & Feedback Module**
   - Survey widget to collect user interest and feature wishlists (e.g. automated delay claims, family profiles).
   - Real-time feedback feed with sample tester reviews.
