# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Etiqa Instant Travel Insurance

## Objective
Create a compelling launch-style brag video demonstrating the **Instant Insurance Toggle** journey from bank transaction detection to 1-tap coverage.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape (1920x1080) and vertical (1080x1920)
- Duration: 20 seconds (5 scenes)

## Source Material
- Live Prototype: `https://etiqa-instant-travel-insurance-32077444844.us-west1.run.app`
- Product Name: **Etiqa Instant Travel Insurance (Instant Insurance Toggle)**
- Tagline: *"Forward. Confirm. Done."*

5. `16.5s - 20.0s`: Active policy confirmation & peace of mind.
