# Brag Plan: Etiqa Instant Travel Insurance (Instant Insurance Toggle)

## 9-Question Planning Rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   An AI-powered bank integration where customer flight bookings trigger an instant prompt to forward their airline itinerary from Gmail to `travel@etiqa.com.my`, which AI parses in 0.8s to provide a ready, 1-tap quote for under RM 80.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "Skip the 30-field form. Forward your airline booking email and get protected in 1 tap for under RM 80."

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   A familiar Gmail inbox view receiving a Maybank flight alert, followed by hitting "Forward" to Etiqa, and receiving a pre-filled instant quote in seconds.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   - Scene 1: Gmail notification from Maybank detecting the Tokyo flight.
   - Scene 2: AirAsia flight itinerary forwarded to `travel@etiqa.com.my`.
   - Scene 3: Etiqa AI scanner extracting trip parameters in 0.8s + instant reply email.
   - Scene 4: Pre-filled 1-Tap checkout sheet (TripCare 360 Plan for RM 48.00).
   - Scene 5: Verified policy badge `ETQ-2024-8842` with automated flight delay payouts.

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   16 seconds (5 high-energy beats).

6. **What tone fits best?**
   - **Preset**: `polished` / `app-store`
   - **Creative Direction**: High-speed, frictionless fintech journey showing natural email habits turned into instant insurance.

7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Upbeat modern electronic beat with tactile UI sounds: email chime, send swoosh, AI scanner chirp, haptic tap, and victory fanfare.

8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "Why are we still typing passport numbers into 30-field forms for travel insurance? Forward your flight confirmation to travel@etiqa.com.my and get covered in 1 tap for under RM 80! ✈️🛡️"

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Flight detected in Gmail ➔ Hit Forward to travel@etiqa.com.my ➔ AI replies in 0.8s ➔ 1-Tap Pay RM 48 with MAE.
